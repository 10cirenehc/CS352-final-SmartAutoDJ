SmartAutoDJ

Structure-aware automatic DJ transitions.

Given two songs, SmartAutoDJ analyzes tempo, beats, downbeats, and song structure, then builds a short transition that is musically smarter than a plain crossfade — optionally layering in an AI-generated bridge. Everything below is benchmarked across three tiers so you can hear what each piece buys us.

  • Tier 1 — Baseline crossfade
  • Tier 2 — Beat-aligned
  • Tier 3 — AI-enhanced bridge

Demos

Each card plays the same song pair transitioned three ways. Audio is trimmed to a window around the transition so you hear the handoff immediately. Metrics and plots are pulled straight from the pipeline's output sidecars.

Course topics, where they show up

SmartAutoDJ is a Music Perception final project. Each module demonstrates a course concept:

Course topicWhere it shows up here
Loudness & Amplitude Loudness-continuity metric (max dB jump across the transition) and the volume-fade curves in each fades plot.
Fourier Transforms & Spectrograms The per-tier spectrogram images of each rendered transition.
Convolution & Filtering (EQ) Equal-power bass-swap EQ in the beat-aligned tiers — visible as the EQ curves in the fades plot.
Self-Similarity & Structure Fused RMS/MFCC/chroma/onset novelty drives cue-point selection — see the structure plot (tiers 2 & 3).
MFCCs & Chromagrams / Pitch Krumhansl key estimation and harmonic compatibility — the key labels shown on each demo card.
Deep Learning & Embeddings Neural beat tracking (beat_this) and the tier-3 AI bridge generated with MusicGen — its text prompt is shown on the tier-3 block.
