How it works

From two songs to one musical handoff.

Stage 01

Analyze musical structure

SmartAutoDJ extracts tempo, beat grids, downbeats, and section cues for both tracks. The pipeline can use allin1 for richer structure or fall back to librosa so the demo stays reproducible.

Three comparison tiers

Each tier adds one layer of musical intelligence.

Tier 1

Baseline crossfade

A direct linear overlap from the tail of song A into the head of song B. It sets the control condition for every comparison.

  • No region selection
  • No tempo matching
  • Plain full-band fades
Tier 2

Beat-aligned transition

Selects a musically meaningful overlap, stretches B toward A's tempo, aligns downbeats, and uses equal-power fades with bass swap filtering.

  • Downbeat-aware overlap
  • Tempo stretch ratio
  • Low-frequency handoff
Tier 3

AI-enhanced bridge

Adds a short riser, fill, swell, or pad underneath the tier 2 mix. Static generated clips can be dropped into the repo later.

  • Trimmed to transition length
  • Normalized under the mix
  • Rendered with the same plan

What the sidecar explains

Every render carries evidence.

Beat alignment error Mean |delta t|

Shows how tightly matched beats land across the overlap.

Tempo match quality BPM before -> after

Shows how much time stretching reduces the tempo gap.

Loudness continuity Max dB jump

Checks whether the transition creates abrupt level changes.
